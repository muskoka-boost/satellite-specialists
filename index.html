Satellite TV

Where cable never came down the road, satellite still works, as long as the dish is aimed right and stays that way.

We install, align and repair dishes for Bell, Shaw Direct and DirecTV. We mount solid, peak the signal for your exact coordinates, run clean cable to the receiver and set up the TV before we pack up. Dish knocked out by a storm or a falling branch? We re-align it.

  • New installs and provider switches
  • Re-alignment after wind, snow and ice
  • Homes, seasonal cottages and businesses
  • Starlink dish mounting and cable routing

Cell signal boosters

Granite, heavy timber and twenty kilometres to the nearest tower. That is why your phone gives up at the cottage.

A booster takes the weak signal outside, amplifies it and rebroadcasts it inside. It only works if the outside antenna is placed and aimed properly, so we test before we drill and test again before we leave. It is our most requested job in Muskoka.

  • Rogers, Bell, Telus and the carriers that ride on them
  • Log cabins, stone cottages and timber frames
  • Single rooms to whole buildings, home or commercial

We don't leave until it works.

A handyman mounts the dish and hopes. We do this every day, with meters, and we prove the signal before we go.

  1. Call or textTell us the address and what's wrong. A photo of the dish or the room with no signal helps. Same-week visits are usual.
  2. Survey on siteWe take readings, check sightlines through the trees and find where the signal really is before anything gets mounted.
  3. Install cleanSolid mounts, sealed entries, and cable run like it belongs there, not stapled across the siding.
  4. Test, then leaveWe verify the picture, the bars or the camera feeds with you standing there. No callbacks, no guessing.
